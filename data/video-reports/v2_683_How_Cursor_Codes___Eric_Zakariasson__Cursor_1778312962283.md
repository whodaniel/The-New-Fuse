## Summary
Eric, an engineer at Cursor, discusses building a 'software factory' using AI agents. He outlines Dan Shapiro's six levels of autonomy for AI in software development, from basic autocomplete (level 1) to a fully autonomous 'dark factory' (level 6) where agents operate with minimal human intervention. He emphasizes the benefits: increased throughput, 24/7 operation, consistent outputs through assembly-line-like processes, and better leverage of human taste/oversight. He notes current challenges with model non-determinism and the need for guardrails, suggesting these will improve as models advance.

## Key Points
- Six levels of AI autonomy in software development: 1) Spicy autocomplete, 2) Pair programmer, 3) AI generates majority of code with human review, 4) Human delegates and reviews outputs, 5) Human manages intent/instructions, 6) Dark factory (fully autonomous black box)
- Software factory benefits: higher throughput, 24/7 agent operation without human biological needs, consistent outputs via assembly line patterns, better leverage of human taste
- Current state: Most users are between levels 2-3; speaker is at level 4 for most projects
- Challenge: Model non-determinism requires building guardrails and better factory design
- Reference to Dan Shapiro's blog post from early 2024 defining these autonomy stages
