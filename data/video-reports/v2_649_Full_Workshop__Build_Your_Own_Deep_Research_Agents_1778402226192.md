This presentation dives into a practical approach to AI engineering for high-quality content creation, contrasting it with low-effort 'AI slop'. The core of the talk is a multi-agent system designed to automate deep research and writing, generating technical articles with code and images. The speaker meticulously differentiates between deterministic 'workflows' – consisting of chained prompts, routers, and loops – and true 'agents,' which have the autonomy to react and adapt to environmental feedback. The focus is on finding the simplest architecture that accomplishes the task at hand, weighing autonomy against control and cost.
