## Summary
Conference talk introducing the concept of 'Context is the New Code' or Context Development Life Cycle (CDLC). The speaker, Patrick, draws parallels between the DevOps movement (2009) and the current shift in AI-assisted development where context (prompts, skills, workflows) becomes as important as traditional code.
