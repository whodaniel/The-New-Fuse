TNF DACC Axes — Role ⊥ Platform

Canonical visualization for the corrected TNF taxonomy. Hierarchy seats and fulfillment platforms are independent. The baton identity ORCHESTRATOR-{timestamp} is held only by master-clock, never by a platform name such as Antigravity.

Baton identity

Who holds the clock?

ORCHESTRATOR-{ts} from master-clock.ts. One active session. Handoffs to prior sessions are migrated onto the current baton.

daccRole

Where do they sit?

director · orchestrator · broker · worker · participant · coordinator · bridge. Infra seats are excluded from broker worker dispatch by seat, not by platform.

workerAction / caps

What work can they do?

Coordination work (orchestration capabilities, workerAction: orchestrator) can be assigned to any worker on any platform without claiming the baton.

platform

Which surface runs them?

antigravity · claude · gemini · pi · jules · vscode · browser · tnf-runtime · master-clock. Product/CLI surface only — not a hierarchy label.

Anti-patterns this viz corrects

Wrong implication Correct reading
“Antigravity is the Orchestrator” Antigravity is a platform. It may register as worker (default) with orchestration capabilities. Baton = master-clock.
“orchestrator-antigravity owns DACC” Historical demo IDs. Live baton is ORCHESTRATOR-{ts}; registry role and platform remain independently assignable.
“Broker only routes to Antigravity for orchestration tasks” Broker routes to workers by capability / fulfillment. Infra daccRole values are excluded regardless of platform.
“Relationship graph cluster=orchestration ⇒ baton holder” Cluster labels domain/work affinity. They are not DACC seats. See taxonomy metadata on the agent-relationship-graph artifact.
