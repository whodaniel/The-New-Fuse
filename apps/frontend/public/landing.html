❓ FAQ

Questions about
Lifetime Deals

What happens when I hit my message limit?

You'll receive a notification when you approach your limit. You can upgrade to a higher tier or wait for your monthly reset. We never cut off service unexpectedly.

Is the lifetime deal really lifetime?

Yes. Pay once and have access for life. This includes all updates and new features we release. The only limitation is your message cap per month.

Can I self-host if I want?

Absolutely. The open-source runtime (fuse-open-runtime on GitHub) is 90% of the platform. Self-host it anywhere that runs Node.js, Redis, and PostgreSQL.

What MCP servers are supported?

Any server implementing the Model Context Protocol. We support the official MCP servers (filesystem, Brave Search, Slack, etc.) plus custom servers you build.

What's the difference between hosted and self-hosted?

Hosted (app.thenewfuse.com) gives you zero-setup cloud infrastructure, automatic updates, and managed Redis/PostgreSQL. Self-hosted gives you full control but requires your own infrastructure.

Do unused messages roll over?

No, message caps reset monthly. Unused messages don't accumulate. This allows us to offer predictable pricing while keeping the service sustainable.
